## Evidence

| Requirement | Evidence |

| ACK only after the storage commit (§10, E5, E6) | `v1-durable-delivery.test.ts`: all 17 durable accepted shared streams replayed through the pipeline with an in-memory storage stand-in; the `A:` steps and final cursors match and every ACK passes the producer-side `AckValidator`; in each stream's log every ACK follows the commit that covers it.
