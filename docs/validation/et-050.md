# ET-050 gear display mode validation

Card: [ET-050](https://trello.com/c/y4TUoF6u). Implementation date: October 25, 2026. Decisions GD1–GD8 are in [DESIGN-DECISIONS.md](../../DESIGN-DECISIONS.md#et-050-gear-display-mode--october-25-2026); the settings format is in [settings](../settings.md#format-v9-gear-display-et-050).

## Candidate and behavior

Based on main `22aed05` (ET-045f). Product and unit-test commit `ac736ce`, runtime-harness commit `b86bbf9`; this document and the other documentation follow in a separate commit.

- A per-game gear display mode, `automatic` or `manual`, default `automatic`, on Settings > Units (EN/RU card, one labelled radio group per game).
- telemetry-core's `formatGear(index, mode)`: N and R1, R2… in both modes; forward g is `g` in automatic and `ceil(g / 2)` plus L (odd) or H (even) in manual.
- The Live instrument, the Live details row and all four overlay layouts use the displayed game's mode. The protocols, SQLite, samples, journal and export keep the raw integer gear.
- Settings format v9 with a v8-to-v9 in-memory migration and no startup write; version 10 or later is newer-version.
- One trusted setter, `settings:set-gear-display`, through the shared durable queue.
- The strip's gear reserve is mode-independent (`R0`, `0H`, `0L`); window sizes and the row model are unchanged.

## Remaining acceptance

These checks need the owner or a real environment and remain **unverified**:

- ETS2 and ATS, single-player and TruckersMP, windowed and borderless: whether the L/H labels match the in-game dashboard for the owner's actual gearboxes. Mismatches are expected for 12+2 crawler boxes, Eaton Fuller 10-, 13- and 18-speed layouts and other non-uniform splitters, because the formula is the card's uniform simplification (GD2).
- Update timing of the gear text during shifts, and readability of `8H` and `9H` in all four layouts at 50%, 100% and 300%.
- Frame-time impact beside a running game.
- Narrator reading of the Gear display card in EN and RU, and Windows display scaling.
- A real installed update from a v8 settings file to v9, and the owner's acceptance of the labels.
