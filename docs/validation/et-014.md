With the truck unavailable for the same reason at both readings, a change was recorded across a disconnection although the truck was unknown on both sides. Coordinator decision: the connection-hole delta needs a known truck at both readings as well as equal keys; `odometerStep` within a connection is unchanged. Fixed in `4e3b89f`.

**RED.** The new case (truck not observed on both sides of a disconnection, odometer 1,000 then 1,300 m) failed on `4cdb2e6`: 1 of 35 accounting tests, `odometerDeltaM` 300 instead of null. It passes after the fix.

## Seeded-stream timeout flake (October 17, 2026)

Hosted Windows CI [run 38984923957](https://github.com/senukartur/ets2telemetry/actions/runs/38984923957) (PR #99) failed `seeded random streams (T18, REG-005)` in `tests/unit/trip-reducer-replay.test.ts` with "Test timed out in 5000ms" after 5,240 ms. The test is a deterministic, CPU-bound, bounded loop: 150 seeds of 120 steps each, 19,901 reducer steps in total. It used Vitest's default 5 s timeout.

**Measured** on `a8745f9` (local 24-thread machine): 2,281–2,358 ms in five runs alone, and 4,371 ms while a full `npx vitest run` and four busy-loop processes ran in parallel. About 0.7 s is reducer steps and 0.3 s the effect-model checks; the rest is message construction and assertions, with no fixture rebuilt per iteration.

**Fix.** The test now has an explicit 30 s timeout with a comment, the pattern other long deterministic tests already use. Seeds, steps and assertions are unchanged. No other test in this file, `trip-reducer.test.ts`, `trip-simulator-replay.test.ts` or `simulator-v1-replay.test.ts` used more than 120 ms (under 3 % of the default timeout), so none was changed; `tests/storage` has no replay file.
