The owner added item 7 during ATS S2 and clarified it on October 23, 2026: reserve a three-digit speed from the start, so that 9↔10 and 99↔100 never resize the speed section or move its neighbours, and give RPM a stable width that covers 999↔1000. Item 8 asks for slightly smaller values, balanced against icons and labels, in every layout.

## Causes

- **Item 7, moving strip.** A strip item is an `inline-flex` box with only a maximum width, and its value sizes to its text. The strip row is a centred, wrapping flex line, so one more digit (about 0.67 em, about 15 px at the old 22 px speed) widens the item and centring moves every item on the line. Tabular figures were already on (`.overlay { font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums }`); the missing piece was a minimum width.
- **Russian strip RPM.** The RPM value was one text with its unit, at 20 px and weight 800. "0000 об/мин" needs about 6.67 em, about 133 px, plus the 16 px icon and the 6 px gap: about 155 px against the 130 px cap, so the unit ended in an ellipsis.
- **Item 2, Cluster RPM.** The gauge centre was a centred flex column of a 40 px speed, its unit, the gear badge and a 12 px "n rpm" line without a width limit, about 104 px high. The RPM line sat about 36 to 52 px below the centre, where the RPM ring's inner edge, (40 − 1.5) × 186 / 120 ≈ 59.7 CSS px from the centre, leaves a chord of about 59 px; "2528 об/мин" at 12 px is about 75 px wide.

Font measurements of the bundled Onest variable font: its digits are proportional by default ("1" 0.363 em, "0" 0.665 em); with `tnum` every digit is 0.672 em at weight 400 and 0.673–0.674 em at 600–800. The hyphen is 0.458 em, the em dash 0.816 em, "N" 0.766 em and "R" 0.690 em at weight 700. The `ch` unit (the default "0", 0.665 or 0.659 em) is narrower than a tabular digit, so a `3ch` minimum width would still change by about half a pixel; the reserve therefore uses the same font as the value.

## Change

- **Stable widths (W1).** The view model (`overlay-view.ts`) gives speed the reserve `000`, gear `R0` (1.363 em, wider than `00` at 1.346 em) and RPM `0000`, and no other section. The renderer writes it to the value element as `data-reserve`. In the strip only, `::after` renders that text as an invisible zero-height block in the same font, so the value is as wide as the wider of its text and its reserve, and digits align right so the unit stays put. The reserve is CSS content: the value's `textContent`, the existing smoke texts and the accessibility tree are unchanged. Speeds 0 to 999 including reverse ("-99" is 1.81 em) and the dash, gears N, 1–99 and R1–R9, and RPM 0 to 9999 fit; a wider value grows under the item's cap and ellipsis (W3: fuel, range, consumption, job and time keep their content width).
- **RPM number and unit (W2).** For RPM only, the view splits telemetry-core's fixed `" rpm"` suffix into the number and a localized unit ("rpm" / "об/мин"), as speed and fuel already are; any other text stays whole without a unit, and an unavailable RPM keeps its unit beside the dash. No number is parsed. Russian "0000 об/мин" in the strip now needs about 114 px of its 130 px cap.
- **Typography (W4).** Values are slightly smaller and never below the 12 px labels and units:

  | Element | Before | After |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Strip value | 20 px / 800 | 17 px / 700 |
  | Strip speed | 22 px / 800 | 19 px / 700 |
  | Card hero speed (3, 2 or 1 parts) | 40 / 28 / 18 px, 800 | 34 / 24 / 16 px, 700 |
  | Card gear (alone) | 22 (18) px | 19 (16) px |
  | Card row values and hero RPM | 13 px | 12 px |
  | Cluster gauge speed | 40 px / 600, line height 1.05 | 34 px / 600, line height 1 |
  | Cluster gauge speed unit | line height 1.3 | line height 1.1 |
  | Cluster gear | 16 px | 14 px, line height 1.2, padding 1 px 8 px |
  | Cluster RPM | "n rpm" 12 px / 400 | number 12 px / 500 muted and unit 11 px, line height 1.2, 3 px apart |
  | Cluster panel values | 12 px / 600 | unchanged |
  | HUD values / speed | 14 / 16 px | 13 / 15 px |

  Labels (12 px), units and details (12 px), tags (11 px), the game name (12 px) and icons (16 px in the strip, 14 px in rows) are unchanged.
- **Cluster centre (W5).** The centre is a three-row grid (`minmax(0, 1fr) auto minmax(0, 1fr)`, 3 px apart): the speed in the middle row, exactly centred; the gear badge at the bottom of the first row, at most 64 px wide; the RPM at the top of the last row, at most 80 px wide, its number shrinking with an ellipsis before the unit. The speed part is at most 104 px wide. The RPM ring's track carries `data-testid="overlay-rpm-track"`. Ring radii, strokes and the 3000 rpm display scale are unchanged.
- **No geometry change (W6).** `STRIP_ROW_HEIGHT_CSS`, `STRIP_ITEM_MAX_WIDTH_CSS`, the overlay-rows constants, `OVERLAY_NOMINAL_SIZE` and every window size are unchanged: the strip with every section is still 1280 × 116 and the card, cluster and HUD 300 × 384, 440 × 267 and 320 × 354 at scale 1. The window size never depends on values; fit refusal and ellipsis are unchanged.
- **Unchanged (W8).** `OverlayPresentation`, preload validation, IPC, the settings format, presets, the wire, storage, the export, native code and `GAME_HISTORY_RECORDING`.
