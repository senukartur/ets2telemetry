# ET-012d validation: card, cluster and HUD fit

ET-012d, a package of the Trello card ET-012, keeps every enabled section of the card, the cluster and the minimal HUD inside the overlay window. With protocol v1 data (ET-010c) the cluster's side panel was capped at 200 px and clipped game time out of its 440 × 220 window, and the card and HUD would clip at 13 sections, or at 14 sections with a tag. On October 16, 2026 the owner decided to fix the strip first (ET-012c); the coordinator then merged ET-010c and fixed the remaining layouts here. Under the standing delegation of October 15, 2026 the coordinator adopted the architect's decisions K1–K12 ([concept.md](../../concept.md#et-012d-overlay-layout-fit--october-16-2026)). The behaviour is described in [the minimal overlay](../minimal-overlay.md#fixed-rows-et-012d).

## Scope

- `apps/desktop/src/overlay-rows.ts` (new, pure and renderer-safe): the row constants, `layoutRowCount`, `layoutContentCss` and `layoutReservedCss`. Card 48 + 24n, cluster side panel 36 + 21m, HUD 18 + 24n CSS px at zoom 1 with borders, where n counts the enabled sections and m those outside the cluster's gauge.
- `overlay-sections.ts` exports `CLUSTER_GAUGE_SECTIONS` (speed, gear, RPM), which replaces the private set in `overlay-view.ts`.
- Geometry: `surfaceHeightDip(content, border, scale) = ceil(content × scale + 2 × border × max(1, scale) − 1e-6)` is shared with the strip, which keeps its numbers. The card, cluster and HUD are `round(nominal width × scale)` wide and `max(round(nominal height × scale), surfaceHeightDip(...))` high, in `overlaySize`, `overlayBounds`, `placementFromBounds` and `overlayWindowSize`.
- CSS: fixed heights for the card header (20 px) and rows (24 px), the cluster tag row and panel rows (18 px) and the HUD tag row (18 px) and lines (22 px). Rows centre their content and hide overflow. The cluster panel's `max-height: 200px` is removed. Header tags shrink, and HUD values end in an ellipsis.
- Comments only in `overlay-renderer.tsx`, `overlay-controller.ts` and `main.ts`. The controller and main logic are unchanged (K9).
- Telemetry smoke: the card, HUD and cluster containment, formerly review-only, is now asserted ([CI](../ci.md#layout-containment-step-et-012d)). The strip at scale 2 stays review-only, and the overlay smoke keeps only the strip assertion.

Unchanged (K12): settings format v3, preload and IPC, `OverlayPresentation`, the protocol, fixtures, service and native code, `RuntimeReceipt.ps1` and the CI workflow. The REG-016 code paths (click-through, no focus, `showInactive()`, the topmost re-assertion) are untouched.

The same branch also carries the deferred ET-010c wording fix. "the same v0 source continues" becomes "an update still carries a v0 observation" in `minimal-overlay.md`, `live-telemetry-fields.md`, `et-010.md` and a comment in `overlay-presentation.ts` (comment only).

## Decisions recorded during implementation

- **Float tolerance in the smoke comparisons.** On the fixed code, the first development telemetry smoke failed with `Overlay section values are clipped by their rows` for the cluster's RPM (`development-6d8324883f8e40acac6ed1ec89ffee50`). A temporary, uncommitted probe (`development-6f451bc29cd7482d8bc6e50f94d8565e`) showed float noise, not clipping. The value box ended at 221.3229217529297 and its row at 221.32291793823242, a difference of 3.8 × 10⁻⁶ px. Both containment comparisons now allow 0.01 CSS px, which is less than one 1/64 px layout unit. A unit case uses those exact numbers and also requires a 0.02 px overhang to fail.
- **`flex: none` on fixed rows.** The cluster's tag row and panel rows, and the HUD's tag row and lines, also get `flex: none`. Their flex-column containers can then never shrink a fixed row, even if they were ever constrained. This is not in K8's list; it changes no size inside the reserved window.
- **Smoke waits.** Before observing a layout, the driver also waits for the reserved height, within one DIP. The restore waits for the strip's created height as well as its width. The window bounds the driver set are recorded in `layoutContainment.windows` for review.
- **Plan wording.** The plan's note "Card at 960×540 origin {x:324, y:16}" matches no anchor case. Every anchor was derived by formula. 324 is the card's x in the fractional-DIP case, where its y is 27.
- **Extra cases and hand-derived values.**
  - The 500 × 380 case now hides the HUD (it needs 386) and is retitled. A 500 × 386 case shows the HUD at {16, 16, 320, 354}.
  - The containment sweep keeps 472 × 252 and adds 472 × 299, the cluster's new threshold.
  - The HUD at 1.5 is 480 × 531 (354 × 1.5).
  - The presets at 1.5 stay 450 × 540, 660 × 330 and 480 × 480.
  - The cluster with fuel only reserves 57 (36 + 21), and with gear only 36.

### Test changes because the requirement changed

The card, cluster and HUD height requirement changed (K4). With every section, the default, they are 384, 267 and 354 high instead of 360, 220 and 320, and they need 332 × 416, 472 × 299 and 352 × 386 instead of 332 × 392, 472 × 252 and 352 × 352. Tests that place these layouts with every section expect the new values:

- `overlay-geometry.test.ts`: the four anchor cases, the fractional case, the 500 × 380 case (the HUD now hides), the fit thresholds, the scaled sizes, the card's scaled threshold at 1.5, `overlayWindowSize`, the card fractions and the dropped card fractions.
- `overlay.test.ts`: the per-layout anchors, the card re-placement on a layout change, the 350-high client case, and the cluster edit, drag, drop, scale and restart cases.
- `main-boundaries.test.ts`: the created cluster window (at 1 and 1.5), the card after a layout save and the HUD after a late settings read. The test that creates the cluster window is renamed.

Presets that fit the nominal size keep the old values.

## Hosted-runner screen reproduction

The hosted runner's screen is 1024 × 768. To reproduce it, `main.ts` was edited temporarily, without a commit, to create the overlay at `Math.min(width, 1024)`. The application was rebuilt and the development telemetry smoke run, which passed (`development-19b2c6f2b451465896e7b5e0c51747b2`, on `37b6187` plus that uncommitted edit). The results:

- The strip viewport was 1024 × 110, `outside` empty and three section rows.
- The card, HUD and cluster were inside 300 × 384, 320 × 354 and 440 × 268, with nothing outside or clipped.

`main.ts` was restored and rebuilt afterwards. All three layout windows are smaller than 1024 × 768, so Windows does not narrow them.
