## PR-1: the protocol v1 minor-1 contract (October 25, 2026)

Based on main `43c634a`.

### Behavior

- `packages/contracts/src/protocol-v1.ts` implements minor 1. The envelope minor selects the state schema, so `state.gearbox` (B2) is required exactly in minor 1. The welcome's envelope minor must equal the minor it announces, and a hello is always version 1.0. `negotiateV1(input, {allowDurable, maxMinor = 0})` selects min(hello `maxMinor`, the server's own maximum).
- Every stateless entry point (`decodePayload`, `validateServerMessage`, `validateClientMessage`, `encodeFrame`, `FrameDecoder`, `negotiateV1`, `computeClockMapping`) defaults to minor 0. It accepts or writes minor 1 only when its caller passes `maxMinor: 1`, so every current caller behaves exactly as before.
- `packages/contracts/src/protocol-v1-delivery.ts` enforces one minor per connection:
  - `V1Consumer(durable, {maxMinor})` rejects a welcome above its `maxMinor` and any later message at another minor, and exposes the selected `minor`;
  - `DurableAckCursor.beginConnection(acked, minor)` builds ACKs at the connection minor;
  - `AckValidator(session, durable, minor)` rejects an ACK at another minor, and its `probe` method rejects a clock probe at another minor or before the checkpoint.

  Every new parameter defaults to 0.
- The live observation (`validateLiveObservationV1`, `LiveStateV1`) still validates the minor-0 state; the gearbox is projected only by PR-5.
- The plugin, broker, simulator and desktop application still negotiate minor 0. No v0, CAP0, storage, settings, IPC or native source changed, and `GAME_HISTORY_RECORDING` stays `false`.

### Fixtures

- The frozen minor-0 corpus in `tests/fixtures/protocol-v1` is byte-identical: `git diff` shows no change to any tracked fixture, manifest or stream file.
- The minor-1 corpus is in `tests/fixtures/protocol-v1/minor1`, with its own `manifest.txt` (48 payload fixtures) and `streams.txt` (11 streams). The C++ harness enumerates only the top-level directory, so it ignores the new directory until PR-3 reads it. `.gitattributes` gives the new files the same exact-bytes and LF rules as the frozen corpus.
- The layouts in `minor1/checkpoint.json` (ETS2 DAF 12-speed, 16 slots) and `minor1/checkpoint-ats-mack.json` (ATS Mack Anthem 18-speed, 22 slots) were read from the sanitized CAP-01 run9 and run4 hshifter captures in `tests/fixtures/simulator-v1/cap01`. The tables match the accepted plan.
- The worst-case minor-1 checkpoint, `minor1/checkpoint-worst.json`, is 55,394 bytes: the frozen 51,994-byte worst case plus 3,400 bytes of gearbox. The gearbox holds two `not-supported` counts, which are longer than an available 100, and 64 slots of gear −100, handle position 254 or 255 and two-digit selectors under `selectorCount` 6. It fits within 65,536 (D17).

## PR-2: gearbox-aware labels in telemetry-core (October 25, 2026)

Stacked on the PR-1 head `1577547`.

### Behavior

- `packages/telemetry-core/src/gear-labels.ts` adds the pure `deriveGearLabels(gearbox)`. It returns forward and reverse labels for a recognized layout and `undefined` for anything else, so the caller falls back to the GD2 formula. It follows the plan's seven steps; the stated interpretations are:
  - selector bits are classified from forward pairs only, because reverse labels are always R1…Rn (B4) and a range-only gearbox may number its reverse gears one apart across the range bit;
  - a pair whose two states hold the same gear carries no information and is not counted, so a cell whose splitter states repeat one gear gets no suffix (step 6); a bit with no differing pair is still unrecognized;
  - a crawler exists only with a range bit: the one forward position without high-range gears;
  - a final check requires ascending gears to give strictly ascending labels (crawler first, then by number, L before H), which rejects a range or splitter direction that differs between positions and two gears that share a label.
- The crawler names are one constant, `CRAWLER_LABELS` (B5).
- `formatGear(index, mode, labels?)` uses a covered label in manual mode and the GD2 formula otherwise; automatic mode ignores the labels, and neutral is always `N`.
- The derivation is memoized per H-shifter layout object in a `WeakMap`, keyed together with the two counts.
- Nothing outside tests calls it yet; PR-6 wires it. No wire, contract, settings, IPC, storage or native change, and `GAME_HISTORY_RECORDING` stays `false`.

### Tests

Expected labels are written by hand from the plan's tables and the documented transmission names.

- `tests/unit/telemetry-core-gear-labels.test.ts`:
  - the CAP-01 DAF 12-speed from `minor1/checkpoint.json` gives 1L…6H, equal to the formula, and reverse R1…R4;
  - the CAP-01 Mack 18-speed from `minor1/checkpoint-ats-mack.json` gives LL, LH, 1L…4H, 5L…8H and reverse R1…R4;
  - a synthetic 13-speed gives L, 1–4, 5L…8H, also with splitter states that repeat the low-range gear; a 10-speed range-only gearbox gives 1–10 although its reverse gears are one apart across the range bit; the zero-selector fixture gives plain numbers; a split crawler gives LL/LH; a splitter-only gearbox gives 1L…2H;
  - fallback to `undefined` for forward or reverse counts above or below the slots, two crawler positions, a low-only position above other low-range gears, a mixed bit, a bit without pairs, a bit whose pairs repeat the gear, two range bits, a range direction that differs between positions, a gear with two labels, a duplicate pair, selectors outside the selector count and each unavailable part;
  - manual mode uses the formula for uncovered gears (19 is `10L`), automatic mode ignores the labels, neutral is `N`;
  - memoization returns the same result for the same layout object and derives again when its counts change.
- `tests/unit/telemetry-core-format.test.ts` adds a hand-built label map case for `formatGear`.
- `tests/unit/telemetry-core-boundaries.test.ts` lists the new module; its imports are type-only contracts imports.
- Mutation checks, each restored afterwards:
  - disabling the ascending-label check failed the differing range direction case;
  - counting every nonzero delta as a splitter failed seven derivation cases;
  - removing the explicit more-than-one-crawler check failed nothing, because step 5 then rejects the second low-only position for lacking a high range. The explicit check stays as the stated B5 rule.
